# Product branding sources

These assets identify the products offered by marketplace sellers. They do not indicate an affiliation or endorsement of Dollar Store by the brand owners. Trademark rights remain with their respective owners.

- `shahid.svg`: the original MBC Shahid logo from https://shahid.mbc.net/staticFiles/production/mbc_shahid.svg, linked by the official site at https://shahid.mbc.net/en. The original SVG and colors are retained. A native text VIP label identifies the subscription tier.
- `xbox.svg`: the Xbox symbol from https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/f/f9/Xbox_one_logo.svg. The original SVG and green color are retained. A native text Game Pass label identifies the subscription.
- Netflix, Spotify, PlayStation, Fortnite, PUBG, Valorant, League of Legends and Steam: their original SVG paths from the installed `simple-icons` package, with the existing recognizable brand colors. Simple Icons artwork is distributed under CC0; see `node_modules/simple-icons/LICENSE.md`. Brand trademarks retain their separate rights.

Product branding is rendered with normal geometric edges. Pixel sprites are exclusively Dollar Store decoration and are never used in place of these product logos.
